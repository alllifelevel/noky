Privacy Policy

Noky Keyboard (app.allstudio.noky) · Updated November 18, 2026

Welcome to Noky Keyboard, developed by ALLStudio. We built it so your keystrokes, personal dictionary and layouts stay on your device.

share_off
No data shared with third parties Noky Keyboard never sends your keystrokes, clipboard items or usage information to anyone.
cloud_off
No data collected Typing, prediction, the clipboard vault and your layouts all work offline on your device. The app only goes online when you download a font or choose to donate.
delete_sweep
You control your data Learned words, clipboard history and custom layouts stay on your device and can be exported or deleted at any time.

1. Information Collection & Use

Noky Keyboard works as a local input method on your Android device:

  • No keystroke logging: We never log, record or transmit what you type, including passwords, messages and form fields.
  • No personal data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts or device identifiers. No account is needed.
  • No analytics or ads: The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

2. Permissions & Why We Need Them

  • Internet and network state: Used only to download Google Fonts you pick for your keyboard and to talk to Google Play when you choose to donate. Nothing you type is sent.
  • Draw over other apps: For the optional floating keyboard bubble and the no-keyboard strap.
  • Foreground service: Keeps the optional floating launcher bubble running while it is switched on.
  • Biometrics: Lets you unlock the secure clipboard vault with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Biometric data never leaves Android's own system and is never seen by Noky.
  • Start at boot: For the optional auto-start setting.
  • Vibration: For key-press haptic feedback.
  • Notifications: Only when you turn on the quick-toggle notification.
  • Google Play billing: For optional donations.
  • Voice input: The voice key opens your device's own speech recognizer. Noky does not record audio and does not request the microphone permission.

3. Keyboards & Word Packs

Keyboard (.nokyboard) and word pack (.nokydict) files are checked and installed on your device. Downloading them from noky.allstudio.app needs no account or login.

4. Donations

Noky Keyboard is free, has no ads and nothing is locked behind a payment. You can support development with an optional one-time or monthly donation. Donations are handled entirely by Google Play; Noky never sees or stores your payment details.

5. This Website

noky.allstudio.app sets no cookies and has no ads or trackers. It counts how many times each keyboard and word pack is downloaded or liked, as anonymous totals only — no IP addresses, identifiers or browsing history are stored. Which keyboards you liked is remembered only in your own browser. Fonts and icons on the site are loaded from Google Fonts.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Noky Keyboard does not collect personal data from anyone, it is suitable for users of all ages.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
