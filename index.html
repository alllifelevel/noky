About this app

Everything inside Noky Keyboard

dashboard_customize

Build your own keyboard

Place keys, assign layers, define key actions and adjust depth. Import and export .nokyboard files to share layouts.

>_

Presets included

Hacker PC keyboard, Programmer & Code pad, T9 phone pad, Gamepad, Giant 4-button pad and multi-language layouts.

swap_horiz

Per-app auto switch

Hacker keyboard in Termux, QWERTY in chat apps. Separate layouts for portrait and landscape.

palette

Deep styling

Custom fonts and sizes, corner radius, padding, shadows, glass-like opacity, Material themes, AMOLED dark or any hex accent.

fingerprint

Biometric clipboard

Folders for your clips and a secure vault, encrypted at rest and unlocked with fingerprint, face or PIN. Auto-clear timers.

dialpad

Scenario layers

Phone fields bring up a dial pad, number fields a number pad. Try it all in the built-in typing sandbox.

horizontal_rule

No-keyboard strap

Swap the keys for an ultra-thin toolbar when you use a hardware keyboard or want the whole screen.

psychology

Typing intelligence

Typo correction and next-word prediction that learn on your device. Haptics and key sounds you can tune.

new_releasesWhat's new in 2.0

  • Layout Manager with .nokyboard import and export
  • Per-app and per-orientation auto switch
  • Biometric secure clipboard vault
  • Custom fonts, key sizing, shadows and glass styles
  • Scenario layers for phone and number fields
  • AES-256-GCM encrypted .noky backup
